# Course 6 notebooks: From Beginner to an AI Research Lab

One notebook per session. Open them in Kaggle Notebooks or Google Colab.

- Every cell without a NOT RUN label was run once on a CPU on 17 November 2026 (Python 3, NumPy 2.5, SciPy 1.18, pandas, scikit-learn 1.9, PyTorch 2.15).
- Sessions 2 to 6 and 9 contain the lab code from the course document, with the expected values checked by assertions (for example w = 2.97 and b = 1.96 in session 2, validation accuracy 0.974 in session 4, and the interval 70.0 to 72.4 in session 9).
- Sessions 1, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12 are worksheets: tables and templates with example rows, plus small helpers that check your entries (a compute budget, a claims table, a page budget, an email check). Replace the example rows with your own.
- Session 5 uses scikit-learn's 8x8 digits for the runnable checks. The MNIST cell and the nanoGPT lab in session 6 are labelled NOT RUN: they need a download or a GPU.
- Session 6 runs its ablation on a toy Bangla language with a 30,000-parameter GPT so that three seeds finish in under a minute. In the recorded run the ablation made no measurable difference, and the notebook asks students to say so.
- Session 3 writes a small project folder called `my-project` next to the notebook.
- Hour estimates in session 1 are planning figures, not measurements. Deadlines in session 12 are placeholders.
